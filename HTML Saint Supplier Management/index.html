Contract Management

Capture contract metadata, run a quick review of the terms for missing mandatory clauses and non-standard risk, and move it through the approval workflow: a sourcing analyst drafts and submits, a sourcing manager approves. Only an approved contract can carry a purchase order.

Spend Management

Raise a purchase order against an approved contract and send it to the supplier. The contract fixes the counterparty, currency and payment terms, and caps how much can be committed.
